About This Collection

This gallery brings together 58 meticulously crafted AI artworks that visually reconstruct key chapters of Assam's rich history, folklore, spiritual legacy, and traditional architecture.

👑
History & Royalty

Depictions of Chaolung Sukapha, the founding of the Ahom Kingdom in 1228 CE, the Patra Mantris, and Queen Sati Sadhani.

📜
Luminaries & Reformers

Portraits of saint Mahapurusha Srimanta Sankardev, Kalaguru Bishnu Prasad Rabha, Rupkonwar Jyoti Prasad Agarwala, Hemchandra Baruah, and the pioneers of the Assamese renaissance.

👻
Folklore & Supernatural

Visual folklore traditions including the iconic Japi Pindha Bhoot and rural Assamese ghost lore.

🪔
Sacred Sanctuaries & Namghars

The sacred multi-tiered Guru Asana (Singhasan) and symbolic wood carvings (Kurma, Elephant, Lion).

🏡
Traditional Architecture

Classic earthquake-resistant Assam-type houses, verandahs, courtyards (Chotal), and rustic homesteads.
